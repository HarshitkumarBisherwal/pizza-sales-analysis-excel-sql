# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis — Excel & SQL

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes pizza sales data using **Microsoft Excel and SQL** to identify sales trends, customer ordering patterns, product performance, and key business insights.

The analysis focuses on important sales KPIs, daily and hourly order trends, pizza category and size performance, and the best- and worst-performing pizza products.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyze overall pizza sales performance
* Track important sales KPIs
* Identify peak ordering days and hours
* Analyze sales by pizza category
* Analyze sales by pizza size
* Identify best-selling and worst-selling pizzas
* Generate actionable business insights from sales data

## 📈 Analysis Performed

### 1. Sales Trend Analysis

Analyzed daily and hourly order patterns to identify periods of high customer demand.

The analysis shows that orders are highest during weekends, particularly Friday and Saturday evenings. Peak ordering periods include approximately **12–1 PM and 4–8 PM**.

### 2. Pizza Category Analysis

Analyzed sales performance across:

* Classic
* Supreme
* Veggie
* Chicken

The dashboard identifies the **Classic category** as a major contributor to sales and total orders.

### 3. Pizza Size Analysis

Analyzed sales performance across different pizza sizes:

* Large
* Medium
* Regular
* X-Large
* XX-Large

The analysis identifies **Large pizzas** as the leading size by sales contribution.

### 4. Product Performance Analysis

Identified the top-performing and lowest-performing pizza products based on total pizzas sold.

The analysis highlights products such as **The Pepperoni Pizza** among the top sellers and **The Brie Carre Pizza** among the lowest-performing products.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

* Weekend periods generate higher order activity.
* Friday and Saturday evenings represent important high-demand periods.
* Orders show notable peaks around lunchtime and evening hours.
* The Classic pizza category contributes strongly to overall sales.
* Large pizzas represent the largest share among the analyzed pizza sizes.
* Product-level analysis helps identify high-performing and low-performing menu items.
